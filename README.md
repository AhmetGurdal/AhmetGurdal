## 👋 About Me

With a background in **data engineering and financial systems**, I began my journey as a Data and Support Engineer, where I investigated application issues, analyzed financial datasets, and developed an internal monitoring system to enhance operational efficiency.

Transitioning into a Software Engineer role at a finance company, I worked extensively with **Borsa Istanbul’s FIX API**, **TIP**, **OUCH**, and **ITCH protocols**, building robust data pipelines and a comprehensive **Data Visualization System** (frontend & backend). My work included **log tracking**, **data handling**, and managing **Kafka-based data sharing systems**.

Now, as a **Full Stack Developer**, I design and develop cross-platform solutions ranging from **web** and **mobile applications** to **SaaS platforms**, **games**, and **microservices architectures**.
